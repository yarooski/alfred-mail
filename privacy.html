Privacy policy

Who uses it. Alfred Mail is a private assistant used only by its owner's household. It has no other users.

What it accesses. Only the Google data of the person who signs in, with their consent: Gmail (to read, label, mark read, save drafts and, after approval, move to Trash), Google Drive (read only) and Google Calendar (to read events and add approved events).

How data is used. Solely to provide those features to the account owner. Data is processed on hardware the household owns, using a self-hosted AI model. Short notices (an email's sender, subject and category) are posted to the household's own private Slack workspace.

What it never does. Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, not used to train AI models, and not shared with anyone else. The app never sends email and never deletes data permanently.

Google API data. Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access. The account owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; stored tokens are then useless and are deleted from the household's machine.

Contact. Open an issue on this project's GitHub repository.
