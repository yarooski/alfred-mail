Alfred Mail

Alfred Mail is a private, self-hosted assistant used only by its owner's household. It is not offered to the public and cannot be installed or used by anyone else.

With the account owner's consent it connects to their own Google account to:

  • sort and label their Gmail, and suggest clean-ups (moving mail to Trash only after the owner approves),
  • answer the owner's questions about their email and Google Drive documents (Drive is read only),
  • save draft replies for the owner to review and send themselves (it never sends email), and
  • list calendar events and add new ones the owner approves.

All processing happens on hardware the household owns, using a self-hosted AI model.
